SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE BOARD OF INDIA (SEBI)
CYBERSECURITY AND CYBER RESILIENCE FRAMEWORK (CSCRF) FOR SEBI REGULATED ENTITIES (REs)
Circular No: SEBI/HO/ITD-1/ITD_CSC_EXT/P/CIR/2024/113 | August 20, 2024
Issued under Section 11(1) of Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & STATUTORY APPLICABILITY
1. Applicability Categories:
   - Market Infrastructure Institutions (MIIs): Stock Exchanges, Depositories, Clearing Corporations, KRAs, QRTAs.
   - Qualified REs: Large brokerages (>5 lakh active clients or QSB criteria), AMCs/MFs (AUM >= 1 lakh crore), Custodians (AUC >= 10 lakh crore), Institutional DPs, Conglomerate Merchant Bankers, AIFs (AUM >= 1000 crore).
   - Mid-size REs: Brokers (50,000 to 5,00,000 clients), AMCs (10,000 to 1 lakh crore), Custodians (1 to 10 lakh crore), Non-institutional DPs, Issue-management MBs, AIFs (500 to 1000 crore).
   - Small-size REs: Brokers (10,000 to 50,000 clients or <=10,000 with IBT/Algo), AMCs (<10,000 crore), Custodians (<1 lakh crore), Small MBs, Non-individual IAs, AIFs (100 to 500 crore).
   - Self-certification REs: Smallest brokers (<=10,000 clients without IBT/Algo), CIS, CRAs, inactive Debenture Trustees, Portfolio Managers (<1000 crore), VCFs (<100 crore).
2. Implementation Glide Path:
   - 6 Existing Circular Categories: January 01, 2025.
   - Other REs (first-time CSCRF): April 01, 2025.
   - Market SOC deployment by NSE & BSE: January 01, 2025.

FIVE CYBER RESILIENCY GOALS MAPPED TO CYBERSECURITY FUNCTIONS:
- ANTICIPATE: Governance, Identify, Protect, Detect
- WITHSTAND: Respond
- CONTAIN: Respond
- RECOVER: Recover
- EVOLVE: Evolve

PART I & II: OBJECTIVES, STANDARDS AND MANDATORY GUIDELINES
1. GOVERNANCE (GV)
   - GV.OC: Organizational Context
     * GV.OC.S1: Critical objectives, capabilities, services communicated to external stakeholders.
     * GV.OC.S2: Mandatory compliance with IT Act 2000, DPDP Act 2023, CERT-In directions; audits & inspections; SEBI search and seizure rights over RE and vendor infrastructure.
     * GV.OC.S3: Dependency on third-party service providers understood and managed.
   - GV.RR: Roles, Responsibilities and Authorities
     * GV.RR.S1: Board leadership accountability for cybersecurity culture.
     * GV.RR.S2: Developed, communicated, and enforced cybersecurity risk roles.
     * GV.RR.S3: Mandatory CISO appointment reporting directly to MD & CEO (standing equivalent to CTO/CIO).
     * GV.RR.S4: Dedicated IT security budget head monitored by Board.
     * GV.RR.S5: Mandatory signed confidentiality and integrity agreements before employee/vendor access.
     * GV.RR.S6: Cybersecurity training integrated into HR onboarding and continuous training.
   - GV.PO: Policy
     * GV.PO.S1: Board-approved comprehensive Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Policy encompassing CSCRF.
     * GV.PO.S2: Annual policy review by Board.
     * GV.PO.S3: Cybersecurity risk management policy established and enforced.
     * GV.PO.S4: Policy updated to reflect changes in requirements, threats, and technologies.
     * GV.PO.S5: Clear asset ownership and chain of command for approvals.
     * IT Committee for REs: Mandatory for MIIs, Qualified REs, and Mid-size REs with at least one external independent cybersecurity expert; quarterly meetings.
   - GV.OV: Oversight
     * GV.OV.S1-S3: Cybersecurity strategy outcomes and performance reviewed and adjusted.
     * GV.OV.S4: Mandatory Cyber Capability Index (CCI) assessment: Third-party half-yearly for MIIs, self-assessment annually for Qualified REs.
   - GV.RM: Risk Management
     * GV.RM.S1-S2: Comprehensive cyber risk management framework; risk appetite/tolerance statements; risk register reviewed by IT Committee; ISO 27005 guidance; MTTD, MTTR, MTTC metrics.
     * GV.RM.S3: Mandatory scenario-based attack testing (Annexure-E) covering DDoS, malware, application flaws, DNS, brute force, AD attacks.
     * GV.RM.S4: Risk response prioritization based on likelihood and impact.
   - GV.SC: Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management
     * GV.SC.S1-S3: Supply chain strategy, vendor risk assessments, mandatory cybersecurity terms and SLA clauses.
     * GV.SC.S4: Audits of third-party managed systems (IBT, Back office); MII responsibility for client-facing apps (NEAT/BOLT).
     * GV.SC.S5: Mandatory Software Bill of Materials (SBOM) for critical systems within 6 months; includes top-level components, transitive dependencies, cryptographic hashes, licenses, and patch status.
     * GV.SC.S6: Joint response and recovery testing with third-party vendors.
     * GV.SC.S7: Third-party concentration risk assessment to prevent systemic market disruptions.
     * GV.SC.S8: Prohibition of vendor "Kill Switches" in hosted and application software.

2. IDENTIFY (ID)
   - ID.AM: Asset Management
     * ID.AM.S1: Identification and classification of critical systems approved by Board.
     * ID.AM.S2: Comprehensive asset inventory; SLA of 3 working days to reflect asset changes.
     * ID.AM.S3: Strict prohibition of Shadow IT assets.
     * ID.AM.S4: Up-to-date network architecture diagrams (wired & wireless).
     * ID.AM.S5: CMDB implementation for MIIs; ITSM inventory for all REs.
     * ID.AM.S6: Asset lifecycle management.
   - ID.RA: Risk Assessment
     * ID.RA.S1-S2: Periodic risk assessments including post-quantum computing threats (PQC); development of quantifiable risk score.
     * ID.RA.S3: Dark web monitoring, anti-phishing/anti-rogue app services; onboarding to CERT-In Intelligence platform; MII onboarding to NCCC.
     * ID.RA.S4: Proactive cyberspace monitoring for brand impersonation; risk-based authentication (RBA).
     * ID.RA.S5: Risk prioritization and mitigation tracking.

3. PROTECT (PR)
   - PR.AA: Identity Management, Authentication, and Access Control
     * PR.AA.S1-S3: Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP), segregation of duties, Zero Trust Security Model.
     * PR.AA.S4: Default deny access model for critical systems.
     * PR.AA.S5: Maker-Checker framework for privileges; quarterly cleanup of delegated access and unused tokens.
     * PR.AA.S6: Authentication policy; password complexity; hashing with salt (bcrypt/PBKDF2); lockouts after failure attempts.
     * PR.AA.S7: Mandatory Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) on critical systems and internet-facing logins.
     * PR.AA.S8: Centralized log management: collect system, app, DB, network, audit trail logs; minimum 2-year retention (6 months online, rest archival).
     * PR.AA.S9: Account lock policies; deactivation of departed employee IDs.
     * PR.AA.S10: Physical access restricted to critical rooms; CCTV, mantraps, bollards; 24x7 monitoring.
     * PR.AA.S11: Stringent Privileged Identity Management (PIM/PAM); quarterly privileged user activity review; privileged users barred from altering audit logs.
     * PR.AA.S12: Remote access restricted to whitelisted IPs, encrypted with MFA, fully logged.
     * PR.AA.S13-S14: Data disposal and media sanitization (crypto shredding, degaussing, physical destruction).
     * PR.AA.S15: Endpoint security: EDR/XDR, anti-malware, host-based firewalls blocking RPC/SMB, PowerShell and local admin disabled by default, Active Directory backdoor audits.
     * PR.AA.S16-S17: API security: OWASP API Top 10 mitigation, rate limiting, throttling, whitelist connection. Mobile app security: root/jailbreak detection, no emulators, device binding (device + SIM), checksum publication, anti-tampering, no raw SQL.
   - PR.AT: Awareness and Training
     * PR.AT.S1-S5: Annual cybersecurity training; Board member sensitization; customer fraud reporting mechanism on app/web.
   - PR.DS: Data Security
     * PR.DS.S1: Encryption at rest (FDE + FBE layering), in transit (TLS 1.3), and in use.
     * PR.DS.S2: Data Classification:
       (a) Regulatory Data: Core trading, communications, audit records, stored within India's legal boundaries (Data Localization).
       (b) IT and Cybersecurity Data: Logs/metadata without sensitive passwords/keys, exempt from domestic localization only for international SOC/SaaS if approved by Board and accessible to SEBI within 48 hours.
     * PR.DS.S3: Data Loss Prevention (DLP) across networks, endpoints, storage.
     * PR.DS.S4: Blocking admin rights on client workstations; no sensitive data in HTML hidden fields or cookies.
     * PR.DS.S5: Separation of production and non-production environments; DevSecOps; SIT testing.
     * PR.DS.S6: Source code escrow for critical third-party vendor applications.
   - PR.IP: Information Protection Processes and Procedures
     * PR.IP.S1: Baseline configurations; application directory whitelisting; port whitelisting (block by default).
     * PR.IP.S2: System Development Life Cycle (SDLC) / Secure SDLC.
     * PR.IP.S3: Controlled change management with rollback procedures.
     * PR.IP.S4-S6: Regression testing, threat modeling, OWASP ASVS adherence, DAST and SAST scanning.
     * PR.IP.S14: Periodic Cyber Audit by CERT-In empanelled auditors; maximum 3-year tenure with 2-year cooling-off period; MD/CEO declaration.
     * PR.IP.S15: Rigorous testing of SaaS/COTS; functional audits (data/transaction integrity).
     * PR.IP.S16: ISO 27001 certification mandatory for MIIs and Qualified REs within 1 year.
     * PR.IP.S17: Implementation of CIS Critical Security Controls.
   - PR.MA: Maintenance
     * PR.MA.S1-S2: Logged maintenance; secure remote maintenance with MFA.
     * PR.MA.S3: Patch management timelines: High severity <= 1 week, Moderate <= 2 weeks, Low <= 1 month; emergency critical patches applied immediately; virtual patching max 6 months.

4. DETECT (DE)
   - DE.CM: Security Continuous Monitoring
     * DE.CM.S1-S3: 24x7x365 Security Operations Centre (SOC) (Captive, Market SOC, or third-party); continuous log aggregation and SIEM correlation.
     * DE.CM.S4: Capacity management and real-time utilization alerts (storage, compute, bandwidth).
     * DE.CM.S5: Vulnerability Assessment & Penetration Testing (VAPT):
       - Frequency: Twice yearly for protected systems/CII; annually for others; commenced in Q1.
       - Scope: 100% critical systems + 25% non-critical systems (Annexure-L).
       - Timelines: Report within 1 month of test; closure of findings within 3 months; revalidation within 5 months.
   - DE.DP: Detection Process
     * DE.DP.S4: Half-yearly Red Teaming and Continuous Automated Red Teaming (CART).
     * DE.DP.S5: Quarterly proactive threat hunting by specialized providers or internal team.

5. RESPOND (RS)
   - RS.MA: Incident Management
     * RS.MA.S1-S3: Cyber Crisis Management Plan (CCMP), scenario-based SOPs, incident response team (IRT).
     * RS.MA.S5: Collaboration with Cyber Swachhta Kendra (CSK); close bot/vulnerability alerts in 15 days.
   - RS.CO: Incident Response Reporting and Communication
     * RS.CO.S1-S3: Mandatory 6-hour incident reporting to SEBI (`mkt_incidents@sebi.gov.in`) and CERT-In; detailed report on SEBI Portal within 24 hours; quarterly incident reports within 15 days of quarter-end with Traffic Light Protocol (TLP).
     * Press release for high-impact incidents.
   - RS.AN: Incident Analysis
     * RS.AN.S1-S5: Evidence preservation (memory dumps, network PCAP); Root Cause Analysis (RCA) within 30 days; forensic audit within 75 days; incident closure reports.
   - RS.IM: Improvements
     * RS.IM.S1-S2: Post-incident review; update CCMP and COOP; share learnings with SEBI.

6. RECOVER (RC)
   - RC.RP: Incident Recovery Plan Execution
     * RC.RP.S1: Disaster declaration within 30 minutes of critical system disruption.
     * RC.RP.S2: Recovery Time Objective (RTO) <= 2 hours (IOSCO benchmark); Recovery Point Objective (RPO) <= 15 minutes.
     * RC.RP.S3: Half-yearly scenario-based disaster recovery drills.
     * RC.RP.S4: Off-site golden server images; immutable/air-gapped offline backups tested quarterly.
   - RC.CO & RC.IM: Stakeholder recovery communication; BCP-DR enhancements.

7. EVOLVE (EV)
   - EV.ST.S1-S5: Anti-fragility; architectural heterogeneity (multiple OS, diverse logging layers); proactive threat adaptation.
